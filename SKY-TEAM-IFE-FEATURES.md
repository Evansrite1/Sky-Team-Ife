# Sky Team Ife: what the app does

One place for weekly reports, Wednesday training attendance, issues and
solutions, and who is doing what in every office and zone. Free for now.
Works on any phone or computer. Open it in the browser, or add it to the
home screen.

## Who uses it

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Office | The office leader | Files the weekly report, runs the office, shares the member sign-up link |
| Member | Senior Manager, Executive Manager | Checks in on Wednesdays, sees their zone, tracks their own people |
| Director | Runs a zone | Gives solutions to issues, runs the Wednesday evaluation, checks in |
| Super Admin | Runs the whole app | Approves offices and Directors, turns features on, sees everything |

## For offices

- **Weekly report.** Orders, amount, how many people were in the room, niches sold, anything new sold for the first time, and what slowed the office down. A week runs Thursday to Wednesday. Last month and this month can still be filled in or corrected.
- **Estimates.** If a week is missed it is filled with a clearly marked estimate. When the office files that week for real, its own numbers replace the estimate.
- **Issues, one by one.** Each problem is listed on its own. The Director's solution appears right under it.
- **Distributors.** Add names one by one or upload a CSV.
- **Goals and streaks.** Set your own monthly target. See how many weeks in a row you have filed.
- **Insights.** Plain tips on how to do better, built from the office's own numbers, plus how you compare with your zone.
- **Members page.** Your sign-up link, everyone who joined, their attendance, and the names (never the numbers) of the people they follow up.
- **Zone view.** The other offices in your zone and where you rank.

## For Senior and Executive Managers (members)

- **Join through your office link.** Type your name, your sponsor's name, your phone, your email and a password. No waiting for approval.
- **Wednesday check-in.** Type the code your Director gives you. On time before 2:45pm, late after. Not checked in by 6:00pm means absent, automatically.
- **See the whole zone.** Every office, its orders and reports, and the issues with their solutions. Look only. You cannot change anything.
- **My distributors.** Your private list of people you follow up: New number, Talking, Invited, Came to office, Joined. Only you see the phone numbers. Your office leader sees names and stages. Directors see only how many.
- **Step up.** Apply to become a Director. Any Director or the Super Admin can approve.

## For Directors

- **Dashboard.** Check in to the training, see the Wednesday code for your zone, decide Director applications, and go to four places: Issues and solutions, Zones and offices, Evaluation list, Wednesday training.
- **Solutions.** Only Directors and the Super Admin can answer issues. The office sees the answer straight away.
- **Evaluation list.** Every office in the zone with orders, amount and people, ready to download as a PDF for the Wednesday evaluation.
- **Rankings and monthly summary.** Which office is ahead, week by week and month by month.
- **The code by push.** Directors with notifications on get the Wednesday code on their phone at 2:00pm and 3:05pm.

## For the Super Admin

- **Approvals.** Offices and Directors sign up and wait for you to approve.
- **Zones.** Create and edit zones, move offices between them.
- **Features.** Switch each feature on or off from Admin.
- **View as.** See the app exactly as any office or Director sees it. Look only, nothing can be changed by accident.
- **Directors and Sign-ins.** Every Director, and every account with when it last signed in.
- **Activity log, CSV export, announcements.** A record of admin changes, downloads of report history, and messages to everyone.

## A Wednesday, start to finish

1. 1:00pm: check-in opens.
2. 2:00pm and 3:05pm: each Director's phone gets the code.
3. Members type the code and check in. Directors tap Check in on their own dashboard.
4. 2:45pm for Senior and Executive Managers, 3:00pm for Directors: after this, check-in counts as late.
5. 6:00pm: the training ends. Anyone who has not checked in is marked absent.
6. The Wednesday codes page lists everyone who checked in, with the time.

## Good to know

- Week = Thursday to Wednesday. The evaluation reads the week that just closed.
- Notifications work after you press Allow. On iPhone, add the app to the home screen first.
- Forgot your password? Press Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the email.
